Can't find your answer?

We reply to every message.

hello@fixth.ng

  • Do I need a school email to sign up?

    A school email speeds verification up, but it isn't required. If you don't have one, sign up with a personal email and upload your student ID — our team manually verifies you, usually within a day.

  • Is Fixth really free for students?

    Yes. Creating a profile, applying to any opening, and tracking your applications is free. There is no premium tier for students.

  • What makes a company "verified"?

    Every company submits a valid CAC registration number and completes a company profile. Our admin team reviews each one before any listing becomes visible to students.

  • Which courses can use Fixth?

    Fixth is open to every course. Computer Science, Accounting, Microbiology, Mass Communication, Business Administration, Engineering — if a company lists it, you can apply.

  • How do I know a company has seen my application?

    Your dashboard shows a live status for every application — Submitted, Viewed, Shortlisted, Rejected or Accepted. You also get a notification the moment a company updates it.

  • Can I apply to opportunities outside my discipline?

    Companies choose the eligible disciplines for each listing. Where a listing is open, you can apply; where a discipline restriction exists, Fixth will tell you upfront rather than wasting your time.

  • How is my data protected?

    Passwords are encrypted, CVs and IDs are stored on secure, access-controlled storage, and only verified companies you apply to can see your profile. You can delete your account and data at any time.
