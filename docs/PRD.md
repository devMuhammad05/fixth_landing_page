Product Requirements Document (PRD)
Project Information
Product Name: Fixth
Version: 1.0
Platform: Responsive Web Application
Last Updated: 19th of July 2026

1. Problem Statement
Engineering students in Nigeria struggle to secure SIWES and internship placements because opportunities are scattered across WhatsApp groups, personal networks, lecturers, and company websites.
Engineering companies also face difficulty finding qualified engineering students, receiving unstructured applications, and verifying student eligibility.
Fixth provides a centralized platform where verified engineering students discover verified placement opportunities and companies recruit suitable candidates through a structured application process.

2. Product Vision
Build the most trusted platform connecting engineering students with engineering companies for SIWES, industrial training, internships, and future graduate opportunities.

3. Product Overview
Fixth is a career platform built for engineering students and engineering companies.
The platform enables students to:
Create verified student profiles
Upload resumes
Discover verified placement opportunities
Apply for placements
Track application progress
The platform enables companies to:
Create verified company profiles
Publish placement opportunities
Receive and manage applications
Recruit qualified engineering students
Administrators verify users, moderate listings, and maintain trust across the platform.

5. Functional Requirements
5.1 Authentication & Verification
Student
Students can:
Register using an email address
Accept the Privacy Policy and Terms of Use during registration
Register using a school email address
Register using a personal email with manual verification if a school email is unavailable
Complete their profile
Upload a student ID for manual verification where required
View their verification status
Verified student badges are visible to companies.

Company
Companies can:
Register company accounts
Accept the Privacy Policy and Terms of Use during registration
Complete a company profile
Submit their CAC registration number
Await administrator verification before publishing opportunities
View their verification status
Only verified companies can publish opportunities visible to students.

5.2 Student Profile
Students can manage a professional profile containing:
Personal information
Institution
Department
Academic level
Preferred placement locations
Placement availability
Skills
Resume
Portfolio links
Verification status
Students can:
Edit their profile
Replace their resume
Delete their account

5.3 Company Profile
Each company profile contains:
Company name
Industry
Description
Office location
Logo
Website
Social links
Verification badge
Companies can update their profile after creation.

5.4 Opportunity Management
Verified companies can create placement opportunities containing:
Role title
Description
Eligible engineering disciplines
Required academic level
Location
Duration
Stipend information
Companies can:
Edit listings
Close listings
View applications for each listing
Only verified company listings are visible to students.

5.5 Opportunity Discovery
Students can:
Browse placement opportunities
Search opportunities
Filter opportunities by:
Engineering discipline
Location
Duration
Students can view:
Job description
Requirements
Location
Duration
Stipend information
Students can only apply to opportunities matching their eligible discipline where restrictions exist.

5.6 Applications
Students can:
Apply using their profile and uploaded resume
View submitted applications
Track application status
Application statuses include:
Submitted
Viewed
Shortlisted
Rejected
Accepted
Companies can:
View applicants
Review student profiles
View resumes
Filter applicants by discipline, academic level, and location
Update application statuses
Application status updates are visible to students.

5.7 Notifications
Students receive notifications for:
Verification updates
Application status updates
Companies receive notifications for:
Verification results
New applications

5.8 Administration
Administrators can:
Review student verification requests
Approve or reject student verification
Review company verification requests
Approve or reject company verification
Unpublish listings
Suspend student or company accounts
